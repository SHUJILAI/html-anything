Lumen v0.4 — Calendar that defends your focus

Release Notes v0.4 Auto-Blocks · Slack Sync · iOS Widget · Team Mode

Lumen v0.4 introduces Auto-Blocks, an intelligent scheduling engine that automatically reserves 90-minute deep-work slots in your calendar before meetings crowd them out. By reading your existing meetings and working hours, Auto-Blocks ensures you have protected time for focused work — no manual calendar Tetris required.

This release is built on closed-beta feedback from 210 founders over 6 weeks and includes four major features designed to keep distractions at bay.

What's new in v0.4

Auto-Blocks engine

Getting started

You can be up and running with Auto-Blocks in under five minutes.

  1. Download Lumen or log in to your existing account
  2. Grant calendar access (Google Calendar or Outlook)
  3. Set your working hours and meeting preferences
  4. Auto-Blocks will begin scheduling deep-work slots immediately
  5. Optional: connect Slack for notification snooze sync

FAQ

How does Auto-Blocks know when to schedule blocks?

Auto-Blocks reads your calendar to find gaps between meetings and respects your working hours. It prioritizes morning slots (when focus is typically highest) and avoids scheduling blocks during known busy periods.

Can I customize block length?

In v0.4, blocks are fixed at 90 minutes. Custom durations are on the roadmap.

What if I need to move or cancel a block?

Blocks are regular calendar events. You can reschedule or delete them like any other calendar item. Auto-Blocks will not re-create a block for that time slot.

Does Auto-Blocks work offline?

The scheduling engine runs on-device, but calendar sync requires a connection. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

Is my meeting data private?

Yes. Auto-Blocks processes your calendar locally. Meeting titles and details never leave your device.
