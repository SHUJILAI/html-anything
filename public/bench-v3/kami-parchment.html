Lumen v0.4 · Auto-Blocks Slack Sync iOS Widget

Lumen v0.4 introduces Auto-Blocks, an intelligent calendar engine that automatically reserves 90-minute deep-work slots before your schedule fills up. By reading your meetings and working hours, Lumen now protects uninterrupted focus time without any manual intervention.

This release is built on closed-beta feedback from 210 founders over six weeks and ships three major new features: the Auto-Blocks engine, Slack integration, and a new iOS widget.

"Before v0.4, my calendar was a graveyard of good intentions. Auto-Blocks gave me back eleven hours of real work each week — without me touching a thing."
— Beta participant, Y Combinator W24

What's new in v0.4

Auto-Blocks engine New

  • Runs entirely on-device using a heuristic algorithm plus a small language model.
  • Analyzes your calendar and working hours to find optimal deep-work windows.
  • Automatically blocks 90-minute slots before meetings crowd your day.
  • No manual scheduling required — it works while you sleep.

Slack snooze sync

  • Automatically silences Slack notifications during active deep-work blocks.
  • Prevents context-switching and meeting pings from interrupting focus.
  • Respects your block boundaries without requiring manual do-not-disturb setup.

iOS widget

  • Displays your next scheduled deep-work block at a glance.
  • Shows a countdown timer until the block begins.
  • Quick access directly from your home screen.

Team mode

  • View your team's blocked deep-work time to coordinate collaboration.
  • See block availability without exposing individual meeting titles.
  • Enables more considerate scheduling across entire teams.

How to get started

  1. 01. Update Lumen to v0.4 from the App Store or web dashboard.
  2. 02. Grant calendar access, if not already enabled.
  3. 03. Auto-Blocks will begin scheduling within 24 hours — no further action required.
  4. 04. Optional: connect Slack for notification sync and enable the iOS widget from the home screen prompt.

For team accounts, invite teammates from Settings → Team and enable Team Mode to share block availability across your organisation.
