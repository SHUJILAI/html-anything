What's inside

Built around the way deep work actually happens

Four tightly integrated systems that keep your focus blocks intact — automatically.

01

Auto-Blocks Engine

Each morning, Lumen scans your calendar and working hours, identifies your optimal focus windows, and holds them before meetings can claim the time. Heuristic + on-device model — your data never leaves your device.

02

Slack Snooze Sync

When a focus block starts, your Slack status updates automatically. Colleagues see you're in deep work without you lifting a finger. No more pings derailing a 90-minute run of real thinking.

03

iOS Home Screen Widget

See your next block, a live countdown, and your daily focus score at a glance — right from your home screen. No app-switching needed to know exactly when your next protected window starts.

04

Team Mode

See when teammates are in deep work without exposing their meeting titles. Reduce meeting-request pressure and build an async-first culture — one focus block at a time.

05

On-Device Privacy

The Auto-Blocks engine runs entirely on your machine. No calendar data is sent to our servers. No cloud processing. Your schedule stays private, full stop.

06

Meeting Overrun Guard

Lumen tracks when meetings run long and adapts your blocks accordingly — reschedule windows are suggested automatically so your deep work survives the chaos of a busy day.
