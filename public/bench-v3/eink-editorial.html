02 / What's new in v0.4
01 / engine Auto-Blocks New

An engine that reserves your best hours before anyone else can.

Reads your calendar and working hours in real time. Schedules 90-minute deep-work blocks at optimal slots. Runs entirely on-device — no meeting data ever leaves your device.

  • On-device ML model learns your patterns over time
  • Adjustable block length: 60–120 minutes
  • Works offline — scheduling engine never needs a connection
