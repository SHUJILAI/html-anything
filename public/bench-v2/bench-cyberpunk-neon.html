html-anything --explain --how-it-works

How It Works

  1. 01
    Paste or type — drop in your rough notes, bullet points, or a half-baked idea. No markdown syntax required. Plain English works perfectly.
  2. 02
    Pick a style — choose from 15 hand-crafted designs, or let the tool suggest one based on your content type and intent.
  3. 03
    Hit generate — the prompt-optimizer structures your input, Claude renders it, and you get back clean, semantic HTML in seconds.
  4. 04
    Share instantly — one-click link, no setup required. Your page is live and shareable the moment it renders.
