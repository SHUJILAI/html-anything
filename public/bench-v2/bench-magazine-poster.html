HTML-Anything v2 transforms messy notes and raw Markdown into publication-ready web pages in under 30 seconds. Built on Claude Sonnet, armed with 15 hand-crafted styles, and free for every internal team during the pilot.

01

What Is HTML-Anything v2?

It is an internal AI-powered tool that turns any rough notes, bullet lists, or raw Markdown into a fully designed, production-ready HTML page. No coding ability is required. No design experience needed. Paste your intent, choose a style, and publish.

The engine is Claude Sonnet 4.6, wrapped in a proprietary prompt-optimizer that auto-structures vague or incomplete inputs before the design layer ever runs.

"Just paste your intent, pick a style, and ship."
02

15 Hand-Crafted Styles

A curated gallery covers every use case your team touches: Doc, Deck, Poster, Marketing, Essay, Technical, Changelog, How-To, Report, Case Study, Pitch, Newsletter, Product Launch, FAQ, and the open-ended Special format for experimental work.

Each style is a fully considered layout — not a loose template — tuned to the reading context it was designed for.

"15 styles. Zero CSS required from you."
03

Sub-30 Second Output

From raw notes to a live, shareable web page in under 30 seconds. Real-time rendering means you watch the page build as you edit — no refresh, no wait. A one-click share link is ready the moment rendering completes.

Iteration cycles that once took a designer an afternoon now fit inside a standup window.

"The share link is ready before your coffee cools."
04

Who It Is Built For

Three teams are first in line for the pilot. Product teams building internal specs and documentation. Design teams prototyping layouts and concept pages without opening Figma. Marketing teams shipping launch announcements and landing pages in minutes rather than days.

If your work produces text that eventually needs to live on a screen, this tool shortens that distance.

"Any team. Any notes. One tool."
05

Free During Pilot

The pilot is open now with no cost and no usage limits attached. Every page you create during the pilot period helps the team calibrate the style engine, improve the prompt-optimizer, and prioritize the next round of style additions.

Feedback submitted through the tool feeds directly into the v2.1 backlog. Your first awkward page is useful data.

"No cost. No limits. Help us shape the future."
06

The Intelligent Prompt-Optimizer

The layer that separates v2 from a plain AI wrapper is the prompt-optimizer — a pre-processing stage that reads your input, infers structure, fills obvious gaps, and rewrites the generation prompt before Claude ever sees it.

Vague bullet points arrive as polished, hierarchy-aware content on the other side. The optimizer is the reason output quality is consistent even when input quality is not.

"Garbage in, polished page out — that is the contract."
Pro Tip
Start with the Special style for any brief that does not fit the other 14. The optimizer will still structure your input — the format will simply follow the content.
